 # Introduction for an AWS DevOps Interview

Use this as a framework and replace every placeholder with your actual experience.

## 60-second introduction

“Hi, I’m [Name]. I have [X] years of experience in IT, including [Y] years working with AWS and DevOps. My current focus is cloud infrastructure, CI/CD, containerization, Kubernetes and automation.

In my recent work, I have worked with tools such as AWS, Terraform, Docker, Kubernetes/EKS, ECS, Jenkins or GitHub Actions, and observability/security tooling. My responsibilities include infrastructure automation, deployment pipelines, troubleshooting, security controls and production operations.

One area I particularly enjoy is automating repetitive operational work and improving deployment reliability.”

## Project explanation flow

1. Business problem
2. Architecture
3. Your responsibilities
4. CI/CD flow
5. Infrastructure as Code
6. Security and secrets
7. Observability
8. High availability / DR
9. Incident or difficult problem
10. Measurable improvement

## Follow-up questions to prepare

- Why did you choose EKS instead of ECS, or vice versa?
- How does a request reach your application?
- How does the pipeline authenticate to AWS?
- Where are secrets stored?
- What security scans run before deployment?
- How do you roll back?
- How do you detect infrastructure drift?
- What happens when a pod is unhealthy?
- How do you monitor SLOs?
- What was the most difficult production issue you solved?

## Important

Do not memorize a project story that does not match your actual experience. Interviewers commonly drill into implementation details. Be precise about what you personally designed, implemented, operated and automated.
